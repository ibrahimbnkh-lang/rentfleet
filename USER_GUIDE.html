1. System Architecture & Overview

RentFleet Algérie is designed as a high-performance Modular Monolith tailored specifically for vehicle rental operators across all 58 Algerian Wilayas.

  • Backend: Laravel 11 (PHP 8.2+) organized in autonomous modules located in backend/Modules/, protected by Sanctum token auth.
  • Frontend: Vue 3 (Composition API with TypeScript 5), Vite runtime, and Vue Router 4.
  • Algerian Specialization: Built-in validation rules for National Identification Numbers (NIN), driver license seniority, 58 Wilaya codes, matricule plates (12345-121-16), 19% TVA, and cash stamp duty (Droit de timbre).

3. Module M00 · Core Administration & Security

Located in the sidebar under Administration:

  • Multi-Branch Management (/branches): Manage physical agency desks (Alger Centre, Oran Aéroport Es Sénia, Constantine Malek Haddad). Branch agents are automatically restricted to their agency's records.
  • Users & RBAC (/users & /roles): Granular permissions per module (e.g. fleet.create, contracts.sign, invoicing.issue).
  • Audit Trail (/audit-logs): Complete activity logs recording timestamps, users, IP addresses, events, and before/after values.
  • Software Licensing (/license): Offline cryptographic licensing verifying vehicle quotas and module activations.

4. Module M01 · Fleet & Document Management

Located in the sidebar under Parc Automobile:

4.1 Vehicle Catalog (/fleet/vehicles)

Complete fleet records with photos, registration plates (matricule), VIN, fuel types, odometer mileage, and daily rates in DZD.

  • 🟢 Available (Disponible): Ready for rental assignment.
  • 🔵 Rented (En location): On the road under an active contract.
  • 🟡 Maintenance (En révision): Under mechanical servicing or inspection.
  • 🔴 Immobilized (Immobilisé): Impounded or accident repair.

4.2 Document Expiration Monitoring (/fleet/expiring-documents)

Monitors the 4 legal documents required by Algerian law:

  1. Assurance Commerciale (Commercial fleet insurance)
  2. Contrôle Technique (Technical safety inspection certificate)
  3. Carte Grise / Carte Jaune (Vehicle registration document)
  4. Vignette Automobile (Annual road tax stamp)
Safety & Compliance Rule
Documents expiring within 30 days are highlighted in amber; expired documents in red. Vehicles with expired documentation are barred from contract assignment.

5. Module M02 · Customers, KYC & Scoring

Located in the sidebar under Clients & KYC (/customers):

  • Individual Accounts (Particuliers): First name, last name, date of birth, Algerian National ID (NIN), phone, and residential Wilaya.
  • Corporate Accounts (Entreprises): Trade Register (RC), Tax ID (NIF), Statistical ID (NIS), Article d'Imposition (AI), and designated authorized drivers.
  • 2-Year Driver License Seniority Rule: Automated validation enforcing the mandatory 24-month license requirement. Probationary drivers trigger warning flags.
  • Scoring & Instant Blacklist: Customer trust rating (0–100) based on rental punctuality. A single click locks blacklisted drivers out across all branches.

6. Module M03 · Booking Engine & Planning Timeline

Located in the sidebar under Réservations (/reservations) & Planning (/planning):

  • Conflict Detection Engine: Instant schedule collision checking against existing bookings, active rentals, and scheduled maintenance slots.
  • Automated Price Quotations: Computes 24-hour cycles, daily base rates, optional extras (GPS, child safety seat), and mandatory security deposits (Caution).
  • Interactive Timeline (/planning): Visual Gantt calendar displaying fleet utilization by branch with color-coded booking bars.

7. Module M05 · Contracts, Inspection & E-Signature

Located in the sidebar under Contrats (/contracts):

  • Decree 04-415 Legal Compliance: Contracts include mandatory clauses, driver obligations, and territorial limitations (continental Algeria only).
  • Digital Touch / Mouse E-Signature: Clients sign directly on tablet or PC screens; stores cryptographic signature vectors with IP address and timestamp.
  • Vehicle Restitution Inspection: Return odometer km, return fuel gauge, damage notes, extra mileage penalties, and automatic vehicle status reset.

8. Module M07 · Invoicing, Algerian Taxes & Cash Register

Located in the sidebar under Facturation & Caisse (/invoices, /payments):

8.1 Algerian Tax Compliance Engine

  • TVA (VAT) 19.00%: Computed on net pre-tax amounts (HT).
  • Droit de Timbre Fiscal (Cash Stamp Duty):
    • Applicable only for cash payments (cash).
    • Rate: 1.00% of TTC base amount, bounded between 20 DZD and 2,500 DZD.
    • For non-cash payments (Cheque, Bank transfer, CIB/Edahabia): Stamp duty is legally 0.00 DZD.
  • Amount in Words in French: Generated automatically on invoices (e.g. « Arrêtée la présente facture à la somme de... »).

8.2 Cash Register & Official Receipts (/payments)

Real-time tracking of cash in drawer, cheques, wire transfers, and CIB cards. Includes printable official A5 cash receipt vouchers with counterfoil stubs.
